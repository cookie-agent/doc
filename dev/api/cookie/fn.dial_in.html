    id: &str,

Expand description

Dials in to the daemon at daemon as runtime id and serves it, connecting again after every drop or refusal for now with a growing wait, until stopped. A connection that has carried nothing, not even a pong to its keepalive pings, for DIAL_IN_DEAD_AFTER counts as a drop, so one that died without a close is noticed. A refusal for good, such as a configured or reserved ID or a wrong token, ends it with the daemon’s reason.
