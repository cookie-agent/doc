Expand description

Runs a click on an asset’s control, target the element whose data-action it is and clicked the element clicked:

  • a figure’s: asset-full shows it over the page and back, asset-reload runs its page again, asset-show-all uncuts its document;
  • a card’s: asset-panel opens it in the side panel, asset-deck opens the media viewer at the card, to step through its gallery’s pictures and videos, asset-download saves it, asset-copy copies its handle;
  • asset-chip, a handle in a reply, opens the newest card of its asset.
