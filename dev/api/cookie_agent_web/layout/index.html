Where the page is, independent of the browser: the rail’s views, the Settings sections, the history entries browser Back closes, and the press that offers to delete a session row on a touch screen, and where a row’s ⋮ menu opens. The session’s ⋮ menu is crate::commands.

Structs§

MenuPlace
Where a row’s menu stands in a wide window, in CSS pixels from the viewport’s edges: its right edge under the ⋮’s, and its top just below the ⋮, or its bottom just above it when the menu would run past the bottom of the screen there and there is more room above.
Press
A press on a session row: held long enough without moving, it reveals the row’s delete action instead of opening the session.
